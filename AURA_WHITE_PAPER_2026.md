# Aura Sovereign Whitepaper (2026 Edition) 🛡️✨

**The Era of Digital Sovereignty & Hybrid Hybrid Consensus**

## 1. Vision
Aura is more than just a digital currency; it is a movement towards **Digital Sovereignty**. Founded on the belief that every individual should be a guardian of their own truth, Aura provides an autonomous financial infrastructure that rewards both capital commitment and computational participation.

Designed by **Than**, Aura is built for the "Ordinary Hero"—the person who keeps the network alive by simply securing its capital or contributing their background compute.

---

## 2. Infrastructure Architecture: Cloud Sovereignty
Aura 2026 introduces a breakthrough architecture combining high-speed off-chain settlement with immutable public auditing.

### 2.1 The Instant Settlement Layer (Supabase)
The real-time state of the Aura network is powered by **Supabase SQL-native RPCs**.
- **Quantum Mapping**: Transactions, staking, and mining shares are processed with ACID compliance.
- **Latency**: Sub-second synchronization across all sovereign command dashboards.

### 2.2 The Verification Layer (GitHub Pulse)
Every transaction settled in the cloud is simultaneously committed to a decentralized public ledger on **GitHub**.
- **Public Auditability**: Anyone can verify the total supply and transaction history.
- **Resilience**: The GitHub repository acts as an immutable, globally distributed backup of the network's truth.

---

## 3. Consensus Mechanism: Sovereign Hybrid Consensus
Aura utilizes a dual-engine consensus model to ensure network security and fair distribution.

### 3.1 Financial Presence (Proof of Stake)
Users earn rewards by staking their AUR into the Sovereign Vault.
- **Role**: Provides the capital backbone and ensures long-term value commitment.
- **Reward**: 50% of the daily emission (1.0 AUR).

### 3.2 Computational Presence (Proof of Effort)
Users earn rewards by contributing background hashing power via the browser-based miner.
- **Role**: Ensures fair-launch distribution and encourages active network engagement.
- **Reward**: 50% of the daily emission (1.0 AUR).

---

## 4. Tokenomics: The Equilibrium Cycle
- **Total Daily Emission**: Exactly **2.0 AUR**, split between stakers and miners.
- **The 1% Burn Rule**: Every operation carries a 1% Burn Penalty. This ensures that as the network's utility grows, the total supply is pressured downward, creating a supply-demand equilibrium that favors long-term holders.
